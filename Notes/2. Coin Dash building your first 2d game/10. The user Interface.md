## Containers
Godot container nodes automatically arrange the positions and sizes of their child control nodes(inncluding other containers). You can use them to add padding around elements, keep them centered, or arrange them in rows and columns.
- if you try to move or resize control that is inside container node you will get an error.
- the controls are done from the container itself.
